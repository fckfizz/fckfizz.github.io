Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 August 2026

ScamKiller ("we", "us", "our") builds an AI-powered tool that helps you identify scam messages, links, phone numbers and screenshots. This Privacy Policy explains what information the ScamKiller mobile app collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. We are committed to protecting your privacy and keeping the data we handle to the minimum needed to run the service.

Information we collect

  • Content you submit for scanning. When you ask ScamKiller to check a message, link, phone number or screenshot, that content is sent to our servers and to our AI processing provider so we can analyse it and return a result. Scan records are associated with an anonymous device identifier, not your name.
  • Anonymous device identifier. We generate a random ID stored on your device so we can show you your own scan history and apply free-tier scan limits. It is not linked to your real-world identity.
  • Subscription information. If you purchase ScamKiller+ or Family, payments are processed by Stripe. We receive a subscription status and identifier from Stripe; we do not collect or store your full card number.
  • Usage analytics. We use privacy-respecting analytics to understand aggregate, non-identifying usage (for example, how many scans are run) so we can improve the app.

How we use your information

  • To analyse the content you submit and return a scam-risk assessment.
  • To show you your personal scan history on your device.
  • To enforce free-tier limits and manage paid subscriptions.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the app.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or tracking. We share data only with service providers who help us operate the app, strictly to provide the service:

  • AI processing provider — to analyse submitted content and generate scam assessments.
  • Stripe — to process subscription payments.
  • Cloud hosting and storage providers — to run our servers and store scan records and uploaded screenshots.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the public.

Data retention

Scan records and uploaded screenshots are retained so you can view your history and so our threat-detection can improve. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see Contact below). Uninstalling the app removes the anonymous device identifier stored on your device.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect data in transit and at rest. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work hard to safeguard your information.

Children's privacy

ScamKiller is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at support@scamkiller.ai.
