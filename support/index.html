Request account & data deletion

You can ask us to delete the data associated with your device and account at any time. To request deletion, email support@scamkiller.ai with the subject line "Delete my data". We will delete your scan records, uploaded screenshots and anonymous device identifier within 30 days of your request. Uninstalling the app also removes the anonymous device identifier stored on your device.
